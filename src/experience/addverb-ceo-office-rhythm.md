problem: "Addverb was scaling rapidly across functions and international offices. The Global CEO needed a way to see strategic priorities, operating performance and cross-functional dependencies without relying on fragmented updates from individual departments. My role in Special Initiatives evolved into acting as a bridge between the CEO Office and functional leaders across Engineering, Manufacturing, Finance and Operations."
why_it_mattered: "As organisations grow, the CEO can easily become the manual integration layer between functions. Without a common planning and review rhythm, competing priorities remain unresolved, decisions are revisited repeatedly and leadership attention gets consumed by follow-up rather than direction-setting."
figure_out: "I started with the CEO's priorities, investor expectations, departmental KPIs and the issues repeatedly escalating into leadership reviews. The objective was to distinguish routine functional work from the cross-company issues that genuinely required CEO attention or coordinated action."
design: "I partnered with the Global CEO on the Annual Operating Plan and designed recurring review mechanisms around KPIs, commitments, dependencies and decision points. The rhythm needed to connect strategic priorities with what functional leaders were actually accountable for delivering."
build: "I prepared decision-ready material, coordinated inputs across functions, ran follow-through on leadership commitments and acted as the interface between the CEO Office and operating teams. When priorities conflicted, the work was not simply reporting status; it involved clarifying ownership, surfacing trade-offs and pushing decisions to closure."
