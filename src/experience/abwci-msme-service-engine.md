problem: "I was asked to help redesign ABWCI at a point when the organisation had ambition and an existing member network but lacked much of the operating infrastructure required to scale it. Digital systems were weak, organisational roles were unclear, knowledge depended heavily on individuals, member engagement was inconsistent and there was no sufficiently structured revenue or project pipeline."
why_it_mattered: "For an institution working across women entrepreneurship and economic participation, the operating model determines whether the mission can scale. A large network has limited value if the organisation cannot communicate consistently with it, deliver repeatable services, capture data, build partnerships or fund its own operating capacity."
figure_out: "I began with benchmarking and with clarifying the founder's vision for the organisation. I also brought the founder, senior leadership and an external consulting team into a structured two-day working session. From that diagnosis, I grouped the transformation around three immediate fronts: digital infrastructure, people and stakeholder engagement, and revenue."
design: "For digital infrastructure, I defined a roadmap covering the website, donor interface, community infrastructure, CRM and application layer. For people engagement, I mapped the different constituencies (members, country and state leaders, partners and the secretariat) and designed a 360-degree engagement approach around information, opportunities and capability-building. For revenue, I worked with the consulting team to design multiple possible revenue pillars and prioritised business-support services, events/market-access opportunities and corporate/grant-linked work."
build: "I engaged digital partners, translated the roadmap into deliverables and timelines, worked on membership and interest-capture systems, began structuring member engagement and developed early business-support and event propositions. In parallel, I started organising the data and impact narrative required for higher-quality corporate and grant conversations."
scale: "The work is designed to move ABWCI away from fragmented activity toward an institution with repeatable digital, engagement and revenue mechanisms. Several components remain under development, so the story should remain visibly marked Story in progress."
transform: "The intended transformation is structural: from an organisation dependent on individual knowledge and episodic activity into one with clearer operating systems, member journeys, digital infrastructure, revenue mechanisms and institutional memory."

takeaway: "Institution-building is transformation while the organisation is still running. You cannot switch the old system off, redesign everything in isolation and restart later. The new structure has to be built while members, partners and programmes continue to need support."
