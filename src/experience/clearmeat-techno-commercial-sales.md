The customer relationship did not end when an order was closed: trials, product usage and technical questions could directly determine whether a customer bought again. why_it_mattered: "When sales teams own commercial conversations but technical issues sit elsewhere, customers experience hand-offs precisely when confidence matters most. For a new product category, that can slow adoption and make repeat business dependent on individual intervention."
figure_out: "I mapped the end-to-end customer journey and looked at the points where technical support, trials, account ownership and cross-sell or upsell conversations intersected. The pattern showed that ongoing account management needed a stronger technical layer rather than being treated as an extension of pure sales."
design: "I separated account management from front-end sales and designed a dedicated techno-commercial model. The intention was to give customers a team that could stay close to trials, understand technical issues in real time and manage the commercial relationship without repeated internal hand-offs."
build: "We created the team and embedded proactive customer support at key moments in the journey, including trials and follow-on commercial conversations. The operating model connected customer experience more closely with the technical capability behind the product."
