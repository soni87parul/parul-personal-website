## How I think about a problem

When I'm given a complicated organisational problem, I tend to work through it roughly in this order:

1. Research the topic and existing context.
2. Understand the CEO / leadership vision.
3. Understand what investors, the board, or decision-makers are actually asking for.
4. Understand what success looks like at the current stage.
5. Speak to the people closest to the problem — business and process owners.
6. Identify the real pain points, not just the reported ones.
7. Map the current state at a high level.
8. Identify what's actually blocking the desired future state.
9. Determine whether the problem is strategic, organisational, technological, process-related, behavioural, policy-related, or a matter of execution.
10. Design a transformation strategy.
11. Define the required changes across people, process, technology, methodology and mindset.
12. Establish KPIs, scorecards and governance.
13. Determine budget and resource requirements.
14. Design the team and hiring structure.
15. Move into execution and transformation management.
